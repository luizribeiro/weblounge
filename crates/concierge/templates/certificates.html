Client certificates

Log in with SASL EXTERNAL: a key on disk instead of a password in your client's config. Your password keeps working everywhere else.

  1. Make a certificate

    Self-signed is fine. ECDSA P-256, ten years.

    {%- call components::snippet(generate) %}{% endcall %}

  2. Get its fingerprint

    SHA-256, lowercase hex, no colons.

    {%- call components::snippet(fingerprint) %}{% endcall %}

  3. Attach it to your account

    Connected to {{ irc.host }} and logged in as usual. CERT LIST shows what's attached, CERT DEL removes one.

    {%- call components::snippet(register) %}{% endcall %}

  4. Switch your client to SASL EXTERNAL

    Halloy reads the certificate and the key:

    {%- call components::snippet(halloy) %}{% endcall %}

    WeeChat wants both in one file:

    {%- call components::snippet(weechat_pem) %}{% endcall %} {%- call components::snippet(weechat) %}{% endcall %}

    senpai can't present a certificate; keep SASL PLAIN with password-cmd. Elsewhere, look for SASL EXTERNAL or CertFP.
