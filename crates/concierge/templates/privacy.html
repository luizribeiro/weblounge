Privacy

What we keep.

Stored

Your nick, your email address, who invited whom, and when you joined. The IRC server keeps a hash of your password and your settings.

Not stored

Members' IP addresses: not in the database, not in the logs. Failed sign-ins are counted per address, in memory, for {{ failed_sign_in_minutes }} minutes.

Messages

Channel messages, for a week, so you can scroll back after a restart. Direct messages never touch the disk: they stay in memory, and a restart forgets them.

Email

News emails are opt-in, and you can turn them off on your account page.

Leaving

Ask an admin and they'll delete your account: your email and sessions go, and your password stops working. Your nick stays in the invite tree, so the people you invited stay attached.
