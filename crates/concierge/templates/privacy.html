Privacy

What we keep.

Stored

Your nick, your email address and whether you've confirmed it, who invited whom, and when you joined. The IRC server keeps a hash of your password and your settings.

Not stored

Members' IP addresses: not in the database, not in the logs. Failed sign-ins, nick lookups, email confirmations and password resets are counted per address, in memory, for {{ failed_sign_in_minutes }} minutes.

Messages

Channel messages and direct messages are kept on the server for {{ history_kept }}, so you can scroll back after a restart, and then deleted. You can delete a message you sent, if your app offers it. Remember that other people's apps may keep their own copy of what you send, for as long as they like.

Welcomes

The first time you connect after accepting an invite, the concierge, the network's resident bot, posts a welcome in #lounge naming you and whoever invited you, tells them by direct message, and sends you a hello by direct message. If you haven't connected {{ nudge_after }} after accepting, it tells whoever invited you, once. We keep a record of when each of these happened for {{ welcome_kept }}.

Uploads

An image you upload, by pasting or attaching it in your app, is public to anyone with its link. The link is a random name too long to guess, and we list it nowhere but your Uploads tab. Where and when a photo was taken, the camera and anything else stored in the file are removed before it's stored, and so is the file's name. We keep who uploaded each image and when, and never where from.

Uploads are deleted after {{ upload_kept }}, and you can delete them sooner on your Uploads tab. Remember that anyone who saw an image may keep their own copy.

Email

We send email through MXroute, our mail provider. A message waits on our server only until it's sent, {{ outbox_wait }} at most; replies and bounces land in our mailbox there.

We keep the links we email you only as a hash: links to confirm your address expire after {{ link_lifetime }}, and links to reset your password after {{ reset_lifetime }}. Emails about new features and planned downtime are opt-in, and you can turn them off on your account page.

Leaving

Ask an admin and they'll delete your account: your email, sessions, uploads and bots go, and your password stops working. Your nick stays in the invite tree, so the people you invited stay attached.
