Privacy

What we keep.

Stored

Your nick, your email address and whether you've confirmed it, who invited whom, and when you joined. The IRC server keeps a hash of your password and your settings.

Not stored

Members' IP addresses: not in the database, not in the logs. Failed sign-ins, nick lookups and email confirmations are counted per address, in memory, for {{ failed_sign_in_minutes }} minutes.

Messages

Channel messages and direct messages are kept on the server for two weeks, so you can scroll back after a restart, and then deleted. You can delete a message you sent, if your app offers it. Remember that other people's apps may keep their own copy of what you send, for as long as they like.

Email

We send email through MXroute, our mail provider. A message waits on our server only until it's sent, {{ outbox_wait }} at most; replies and bounces land in our mailbox there.

We keep the links we email you only as a hash: links to confirm your address expire after {{ link_lifetime }}, and links to reset your password after {{ reset_lifetime }}. Emails about new features and planned downtime are opt-in, and you can turn them off on your account page.

Leaving

Ask an admin and they'll delete your account: your email and sessions go, and your password stops working. Your nick stays in the invite tree, so the people you invited stay attached.
